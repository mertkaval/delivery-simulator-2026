# G2 Visual QA — body shell v2k (sedan_v2_body.glb), re-review of v2d

Independent Visual QA, 2026-10-19. Reviewed: 7 clay renders in `renders/g2_body_v2k/`, `metrics.json` (bounds 1.874 x 4.82 x 1.448 m, 72 090 tris), `dimension_sheet.md`, `ref_left_side.webp`, and the previous report `G2_visual_qa_v2d.md`. Scope unchanged: body shell only (no lamps/grille/mirrors/handles/interior, placeholder wheels; reference wheels oversized by design, 0.66 m tyre is the target).

Measurement basis. v2k renders are framed tighter than v2d: in `clay_left.png` the car spans x 59→1144 px (1086 px) for 4.82 m, so **≈225 px/m** (not the 190 px/m of v2d); ground (tyre bottom) at y = 535; overall height 327 px = 1.451 m, which agrees with metrics (1.448). Front of car on image left. Front/rear views ≈512 px/m (body 1.874 m = x 120→1079). All stations below are metres from the front bumper face.

## Overall verdict: **FAIL** (narrow — two carried-over P1 items remain)

v2k is a large step forward: proportions, the greenhouse/fastback profile, the tail form and the bumper surfaces are now on the sheet, and symmetry holds. It still cannot enter G3 because (a) all four wheel-arch cut edges remain sawtoothed and the arch liners still read as straight-cut discs, and (b) the shoulder character line the sheet calls for is not there — what was added is a belt-edge crease at Z 0.92–0.95 that merges with the belt roll. Two new/escalated P2 surface issues (panel steps at the door shut lines, a fold along the rear-screen base) also need to go before surface detailing starts on top of them.

## Defects in v2k, prioritised

### P1 — blocking
1. **Arch cut edges sawtoothed on all four arches** (carried over). Teeth on the forward-upper quadrant from ~9 o'clock to ~12 o'clock; each step ≈7–9 mm in the orthographic view, very visible in both 3/4 views. Locations in the previous-defect table. Required: a clean circular cut (boolean with a high-segment cylinder, or a projected loop) followed by a bevel/edge loop so the lip is a continuous curve; must read clean at the 3/4 camera distance.
2. **Shoulder character line still absent** (carried over). Sheet: Z 0.80 at the headlamp top (Y +2.0) rising to Z 0.95 at the tail-lamp top (Y −2.0), X ±0.92, passing through the handle stations (Z 0.83 front door, 0.865 rear door). The present crease at Z 0.92–0.945 is the belt edge; keep it as the DLO lower edge if intended, but a separate light-catching break must exist on the door skins. Evidence: clay_left y 330–486 has no gradient break at any sampled x.

### P2 — should fix before G3
3. **Arch liners** (carried over #11): flat disc with a straight vertical trailing cut, hanging below the sill — clay_rear_34 x 613–650, y 640–700; clay_front_34 x 230–245, y 473–490. Make the liner a short cylinder/half-tube trimmed at the arch lip and the sill.
4. **Panels not flush at the door shut lines**: fender and rear quarter stand proud of the doors; the shut line jogs where it crosses the belt crease — clay_front_34 x 633–660, y 405–620; clay_rear_34 x 440–467, y 310–560. Plan outline in clay_top is ≈1.70 m along the doors vs ≈1.87–1.90 m over the arches with the change concentrated at x 250–300 / 740–790. Doors should be flush with the fenders at the shut line (sheet: 1.80 m at shoulder, 1.74 at sill, arches 1.85); any flare should blend over the quarter, not step at the shut line.
5. **Fold along the rear-screen base and C-pillar base dent** (escalation of #10): clay_rear_34 x 760–890, y 380–420 (fold running from the C-pillar across the lower screen to the trunk edge) and x 670–705, y 370–415 (dent in the quarter top beside the rear-door shut line). The lower screen should flow into the trunk lid with a single tangent-continuous transition at Y −1.94 / Z 1.10.
6. **Nose form** (carried over #9): vertical face Z 0.43–0.70 with the hood edge directly above it; sheet wants the most-forward point at Z 0.35–0.55 under a receding hood leading edge at Z 0.78 and a distinct lower-bumper step. clay_left x 59–75, y 378–442; clay_front_34 nose.
7. **Front bumper top-corner fold** (residual of #7): clay_front x 170–205, y 313–345 and mirror x 995–1030 — a short crease where the fender's leading corner meets the bumper top.
8. **No DLO / pillar definition** (carried over #12): the belt crease now gives the glass bottom along the doors, but there are no A/B/C-pillar edges or screen perimeters; greenhouse is still one dome in clay_front_34 / clay_rear_34.

### P3 — polish
9. Rear screen 23° vs 20° sheet (clay_left fit x 840–1000). Acceptable as fastback intent; flatten to 20–21° only if the trunk-lid height can be held.
10. Hood gap lines end in open hood surface (clay_front x 210–270 / 930–990, y 230–290) — carried over #15.
11. Cowl / A-pillar base square notches in plan (clay_top x 185–195, y 180–220 and y 520–560) — carried over #16.
12. Left-view vs right-view length differs by 6 px (≈27 mm) at the tail (clay_left 59→1144 vs mirrored clay_right 54→1145). Rear outline is symmetric in clay_rear, so this is probably camera framing; confirm in Blender that the rear bumper is centred.
13. Rear bumper's rearmost band is Z 0.43–0.79 (sheet 0.35–0.60) — matters for reflector/diffuser placement in G4, not for the shell.
14. Placeholder rims still flat-shaded (135/140 flat faces each) — acceptable as placeholders.
